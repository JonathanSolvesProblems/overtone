Over·tone
Audio description for your video archive — generated in place, on Backblaze B2.

WCAG requires a spoken description track on prerecorded video. Human describers charge $15–$75 a minute, so archives go undescribed or get deleted. Overtone reads each video out of B2, describes the pauses (reading equations and code aloud, not just naming them), and writes the described master, a WebVTT track, and a provenance manifest back beside the original. Nothing leaves your bucket.
